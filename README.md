## A longer summary

My name is Liam Pinchbeck and I'm currently doing my PhD at [Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/) with research focusing on observational dark matter detection. 

My current projects are focusing on the data coming out of the [CTA](https://www.cta-observatory.org), the frameworks that have been set up around it including ctools, cscripts, gammalib and curently gammapy and investigating bayesian analytical methods such as nested sampling and normalising flows. If you have some experience with the CTA or gammapy or gammalib in particular, feel free to reach out. I'm always looking to improve my knowledge of these topics.
